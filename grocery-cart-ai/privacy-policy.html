Privacy Policy Terms of Use

The short version

  • Grocery Cart AI has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Your trips, prices, stores and budgets are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync, they are also stored in your own private iCloud, which only you can access.
  • Price tags are read on your device with Apple's text recognition and Apple Intelligence. Photos are thrown away after reading unless you choose to keep item thumbnails.
  • We never sell, rent or share your personal information.

1. Who we are

Grocery Cart AI (the "App") is developed and published by Archievald Quiambao, an independent developer ("we", "us" or "our"). This policy explains what information the App handles, why, and the choices you have. If you have questions, contact us at business.archievald@gmail.com.

2. Information the App handles

Shopping data you create

When you use the App, it saves the shopping trips, items, prices, stores, budgets, currencies and settings you enter or scan. This data is stored in the App's database on your device. We (the developer) do not have servers that receive it and cannot see it.

Camera and photos

With your permission, the App uses the camera to read price tags and product labels. You can also pick a photo from your library. Images are processed entirely on your device to find the product name and price. By default, the photo is discarded right after it is read. If you turn on Save item photos in Settings, a small thumbnail is stored with the item in your shopping data.

Location

If you choose to find grocery stores near you, the App asks for your location while the App is in use. Your location is sent to Apple's MapKit service only to search for nearby stores. The App does not store your location or keep a location history. The store you pick is saved by name with your trip.

Apple Intelligence

On supported devices, the App uses Apple's on-device foundation model to tidy up product names and pick categories. This runs on your device; your scans are not sent to us or to any AI provider. You can turn this off in Settings.

Purchase

The App is a paid download sold by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment card or Apple Account details, and the App contains no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Exchange rates

To show amounts in your home currency, the App downloads public exchange rates from open.er-api.com. The request contains only a currency code. Like any website, that service can see your device's IP address when the request is made. No shopping data is sent.

3. iCloud sync and backup

If Sync with iCloud is on (it is on by default when you are signed in to iCloud), the App uses Apple's CloudKit to keep your shopping data in sync across your iPhone and iPad and to back it up. This data is stored in the private database of your iCloud account. It is protected by Apple, governed by Apple's Privacy Policy, and cannot be accessed by us. You can turn sync off at any time in the App's Settings, or manage iCloud storage in your device's Settings.

4. What we do not do

  • We do not require or create user accounts.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting SDKs that collect personal data.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information, including for targeted advertising.

6. Keeping and deleting your data

Your shopping data stays until you delete it. You can:

  • Delete individual items, trips or price history inside the App.
  • Use Settings → Erase all data to delete everything. If iCloud sync is on, this also deletes the synced copy in your iCloud.
  • Delete the App to remove the data stored on that device.
  • Export your price history as a CSV file at any time from Settings → Export price history. The export is created on your device and shared only where you choose.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the EU/UK GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act or the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, and to object to or restrict its processing. Because your shopping data is stored on your device and in your own iCloud, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the App. For anything else, contact us and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

8. Security

Your data is protected by your device's security features (such as your passcode, Face ID and data-protection encryption) and, if you use sync, by Apple's iCloud security. No method of storage is 100% secure, so please keep your device and Apple Account protected.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 14, 2026

In short: CapyCleaner checks your photos on your iPhone and never uploads them. We don't collect personal information, show ads, or track you. The only data that leaves your device is an anonymous record of your purchase, so it can be restored.

About CapyCleaner

CapyCleaner ("the app", "we", "us") helps you free up storage on your iPhone by finding similar photos, screenshots, blurry shots, and large or accidental videos. This policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Your photos stay on your iPhone

  • CapyCleaner asks for access to your photo library only to find items you might want to delete.
  • All analysis happens on your device using Apple's built-in frameworks. Your photos and videos are never uploaded, copied to a server, or shared with anyone, including us.
  • Nothing is deleted unless you confirm it. Deleted items go to the Recently Deleted album in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.
  • You can change or remove photo access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

Information we don't collect

CapyCleaner does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photo content, or browsing activity. The app has no accounts, no advertising, and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Purchases

CapyCleaner Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details.

We use RevenueCat to confirm your purchase and restore it on your devices. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (the product, date, and price). This information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising.

Data stored on your device

The app saves a few settings on your iPhone, such as which items you chose to keep and how much space you've freed. This information stays on your device and is removed when you delete the app.

Children's privacy

CapyCleaner is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

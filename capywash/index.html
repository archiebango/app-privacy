Privacy Policy for CapyWash

Last updated: May 6, 2026

Welcome to CapyWash.

This Privacy Policy explains how CapyWash (“we”, “our”, or “us”) handles information when you use the app.

Information We Collect

CapyWash is designed to collect as little personal information as possible.

Subscription and Purchase Information

If you subscribe to premium features, subscription and purchase information may be processed through Apple’s App Store and our subscription management provider, RevenueCat.

We do not receive or store your payment card details.

Device Information

Limited device-related information may be processed to:

  • Validate subscriptions
  • Restore purchases
  • Maintain app functionality and security

This information is not used for advertising or tracking across apps or websites.

How We Use Information

Information is used only to:

  • Provide app features
  • Manage premium subscriptions
  • Restore purchases
  • Improve app reliability and functionality

We do not sell personal information.

Tracking and Advertising

CapyWash does not use third-party advertising SDKs.

We do not use collected data for:

  • Cross-app tracking
  • Behavioral advertising
  • Marketing profiling

Location Information

If location services are enabled by the user, weather-related location data may be used to provide localized forecasts and laundry recommendations.

Location data is not sold or used for advertising purposes.

Third-Party Services

CapyWash may use trusted third-party services, including:

  • Apple App Store
  • RevenueCat

These providers may process limited technical or purchase-related information necessary for app functionality.

Data Retention

We retain only the minimum information necessary to provide app functionality and subscription services.

Children’s Privacy

CapyWash is not directed toward children under 13 years of age.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.

Frequently asked questions

I bought something and didn't receive it.
Open the Shop and tap Restore Purchases. If it's still missing, email me with the date of purchase and the item name. You can find your Apple receipt at reportaproblem.apple.com.
How do I get a refund?
All payments are handled by Apple, so refunds are requested through Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
I got a new phone. Is my progress saved?
Your progress, gold and crystals are saved on your device, not in an online account. One-time purchases (Starter Pack, Drone Pack and drones) can be restored on any device signed in with the same Apple ID using Restore Purchases. Gold, crystals and other used-up items can't be restored if the game is deleted, so please keep the game installed or back up your device.
Why didn't an ad load?
Rewarded ads depend on your connection and ad availability, and there is a daily limit. If no ad is available the reward isn't given. Try again later.
How do I turn off personalized ads?
Go to iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking and turn off tracking for NeoFlight. You'll still see the same number of ads, but they won't be personalized.
How do I delete my data?
Delete the app to remove everything stored on your device. To ask for data held by the services the game uses to be deleted, see the Privacy Policy or email me.

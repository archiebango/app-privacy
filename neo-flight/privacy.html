Privacy Policy

NeoFlight: Space War Roguelike · Last updated October 30, 2026

This policy explains what information NeoFlight: Space War Roguelike ("NeoFlight", "the game") collects, why, and the choices you have. NeoFlight is made by Archie Q., an independent developer ("I", "me", "we"). If you have any questions, email business.archievald@gmail.com.

The short version

  • There is no sign-up and no account. I don't ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • Your progress and settings are saved on your own device.
  • Payments are handled by Apple. I never see your card or payment details.
  • The game shows optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob, which collects device data to deliver ads. Ads are personalized only if you allow tracking.
  • I don't sell your personal information.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (planets cleared, upgrades, gold, crystals, items owned, achievements) and your settings (sound, vibration, tutorial status) are saved locally on your device. This information is not sent to me. Deleting the app deletes it.

Information collected by services the game uses

NeoFlight uses the following third-party services. Each one handles data under its own privacy policy.

Google AdMob (rewarded ads)

When you choose to watch an ad for a reward, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and operating system information, and how you interact with ads. This is used to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, if you allow it, personalize ads.

On first launch, iOS may ask whether NeoFlight can track your activity. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, your advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalized. You can change this any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services

RevenueCat (in-app purchases)

RevenueCat processes in-app purchases for me. It receives an anonymous app user ID created by the game, your purchase history and Apple receipts, and basic device information (such as device model, iOS version, country and IP address). This is used to confirm purchases, unlock what you bought and restore purchases. It does not receive your name or payment details.

Learn more: RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Apple (App Store, payments and Game Center)

Purchases are made through your Apple ID and processed by Apple. If you are signed in to Game Center, your Game Center player ID and nickname are used to save achievements and show leaderboards. Apple may also share crash reports and app usage statistics with developers if you've turned on sharing in your iOS settings.

Learn more: Apple Privacy Policy

Unity (game engine)

NeoFlight is built with the Unity engine, which may collect limited technical data such as device model, operating system and hardware details, to help with performance and compatibility.

Learn more: Unity Privacy Policy

What I don't do

  • I don't collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • I don't sell your personal information.
  • I don't use your information for anything other than running the game, processing purchases and showing ads as described above.

Children

NeoFlight is not directed at children under 13, and I don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, email me and I'll help get it removed.

Your choices and rights

  • Ads: turn off tracking in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Watching ads is always optional.
  • Delete your data: deleting the app removes all data stored on your device. To ask for deletion of data held by RevenueCat or Google related to your use of the game, email me and I'll pass on the request. Google also lets you manage ad data at myadcenter.google.com.
  • Access or correction: depending on where you live (for example under the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, the EU/UK GDPR or California privacy laws), you may have the right to ask what personal information is held about you, to correct it, to delete it or to object to its use. Email me and I'll respond within 30 days.

Security and retention

Data stored on your device stays there until you delete the app. Data held by third-party services is kept according to their own retention policies. These services use industry-standard security measures, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

International transfers

The services listed above may process information in countries other than your own, including the United States, under their own safeguards.

Changes to this policy

If I change this policy, I'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

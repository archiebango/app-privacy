Privacy Policy

Avere · Effective October 12, 2026

The short version: Avere has no sign-up, no ads and no tracking. Your accounts, transactions, budgets and goals are stored on your device. We never see them, and we never sell or share them.

Who we are

Avere is a personal finance app for iPhone and iPad made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Avere. If you have a question, email business.archievald@gmail.com.

Information you enter

Everything you put into Avere stays on your device, in the app’s own storage. That includes your accounts and balances, transactions, categories, budgets, bills and reminders, savings goals, debts, notes and settings. We do not have a server that stores it, and we cannot see it.

You don’t need to create an account or give us your name, email address or bank login to use Avere. The app never connects to your bank.

iCloud sync (Avere Pro)

With Avere Pro, your data syncs between your devices through your own iCloud account, using Apple’s CloudKit private database. It is stored by Apple under your Apple Account and covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy. We cannot access your iCloud data. Sync turns off when Pro ends, and you can also turn it off in the app’s settings.

Purchases

Avere Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never receive your card or bank details.

We use RevenueCat to check whether Pro is active and to restore purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription history for Avere, and basic technical information such as your device type, iOS version, app version, country or region, and IP address. It does not receive anything you have entered in the app. We use this information only to provide Pro and to understand subscription numbers.

Exchange rates

If you use more than one currency, Avere downloads current exchange rates from a free public currency service (served through jsDelivr and Cloudflare). The request only says which currency the rates are for. As with any internet request, the service can see your IP address. No personal or financial information is sent.

Other things that stay on your device

  • Reminders for bills and recurring items are scheduled on your device as local notifications. No push service is used.
  • App lock uses Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode through iOS. Avere only learns whether unlocking succeeded and never has access to your biometric data.
  • Widgets read your data on your device to show it on your Home Screen.
  • Exports are created on your device. You choose where to save or send them.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising and no ad networks.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites, and no analytics SDKs.
  • We don’t sell, rent or share your information with data brokers.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone’s settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. You can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • To delete everything in Avere, go to Settings › Export & Delete › Delete All Data. If sync is on, this also deletes the data from iCloud and your other devices.
  • Deleting the app removes the data stored on that device.
  • To remove synced data from iCloud yourself, go to iPhone Settings › your name › iCloud › Manage Storage.
  • To have your purchase records removed from RevenueCat, email us and we will ask RevenueCat to delete them.

Security

Your data is protected by iOS data protection on your device and by Apple’s encryption in iCloud. You can add an extra lock to the app with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode. No method is perfectly secure, so please keep your device protected with a passcode.

Children

Avere is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the Philippine Data Privacy Act and the EU/UK GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to its use. Because almost all of your data is only on your device, you can manage it directly in the app. For anything else, email us and we will reply within 30 days.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.

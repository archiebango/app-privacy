The app

Avere is a personal finance app for iPhone and iPad made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). It helps you record accounts, spending, budgets, bills and goals that you enter yourself. Avere does not connect to banks and does not move money.

Free and Pro

Avere is free to use with some limits, including up to 3 accounts, 3 category budgets and a 30-day forecast. Avere Pro removes these limits and adds features such as savings goals, debt tracking, multiple ledgers, Home Screen widgets and iCloud sync. The features in each plan are shown in the app and may change over time.
